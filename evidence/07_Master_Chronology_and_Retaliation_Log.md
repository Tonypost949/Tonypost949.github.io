## PART 0 — YOUR ACTUAL STATUS, STATED PLAINLY

You told the earlier session, in your own words: *"im a federal relater i never formally filed bc of fears retaliation would get worse while my mother and i would be homeless."*

That means, legally, you are **not yet a filed relator**. You are a **whistleblower and a potential relator** — a person who made protected disclosures and has not filed a complaint under 31 U.S.C. § 3730(b).

**Why this matters, and why it is not bad news:**

1. **You can still file.** The seal problem I warned you about does not exist yet. Nothing is sealed, because nothing has been filed.
2. **You may still be protected.** Before 2010, § 3730(h) only covered acts "in furtherance of an action." The 2010 amendment added **"or other efforts to stop 1 or more violations."** That language was written for exactly your situation — a person reporting violations before any case exists. Have counsel confirm how the Ninth Circuit applies it, but the door is open.
3. **The clock is the problem, not the seal.** Retaliation claims commonly run on a three-year period. Events from 2023 may be outside it. Events from 2025 and 2026 are inside it. This is why the 2025–2026 rows below matter more than the 2021 rows, even though 2021 is what hurt most.
4. **Do not describe yourself as a relator in any document until a complaint exists.** Say "whistleblower" or "person who made protected disclosures." A defense lawyer will use the wrong word to argue you misrepresented your status.

## PART 3 — THE THREE STRONGEST LINKAGES, RANKED

**1. Disclosure → ejection → warrant → arrest (May–July 2025).**
This is the spine. It is a sequence, it has dates, and it moves in one direction. The defense will say the ejection was for a rule violation and the warrant was unrelated. Your answer is the mailing address: a complaint mailed to a four-year-stale address, producing a failure to appear, on a case where no charges were ever pressed by the corporate victim. That is a procedural departure, and procedural departures are how pretext is proven.

**2. The stale-address mailing itself.**
Independent of motive, this is a documented irregularity with a named custodian and a retained physical artifact. It can be investigated by the DA's public integrity unit or the Attorney General without proving anything about anyone's state of mind.

**3. The 5150 and the medication.**
This is the harm that turns a civil dispute into something an oversight body will look at. The hold paperwork and the medication orders are the documents. Get them.
