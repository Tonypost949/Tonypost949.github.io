# OUTREACH PACK — READY-TO-SEND LETTERS

**How to use this file:** copy the letter you need into an email or a document, replace every `[BRACKET]`, delete the instructional lines at the top of each letter, attach the indicated documents, and send. Save a copy of every send. If you send by email, put the matter name in the subject line exactly as written so the thread is searchable later.

**Before you send anything:**
- Do not send a letter that describes an event you cannot personally confirm. If you did not see it, say who did.
- Do not describe anyone as a criminal. Describe conduct and cite the document that records it. Accusations of crime in an intake letter get routed to a trash can; documented conditions get routed to an investigator.
- Attach documents, not conclusions. The strongest attachment in this case is the November 7, 2023 photograph set and the County's asphalt-cover condition.

**Contact details:** verify every address and email before sending. Offices move and intake portals change. Do not rely on an address in this file without checking the official website first.

**The facts.** The Huntington Beach Navigation Center at 17631 Cameron Lane is a Sprung-structure shelter operated by Mercy House Living Centers on a capped former industrial parcel. ## LETTER E — HUD FAIR HOUSING COMPLAINT (COVER)

**Subject:** Fair housing complaint — failure to reasonably accommodate disability-related hospitalization, Huntington Beach Navigation Center

To the Office of Fair Housing and Equal Opportunity:

I am filing a complaint under the Fair Housing Act, 42 U.S.C. § 3604(f), against Mercy House Living Centers and the City of Huntington Beach, arising from the medical-exit policy at the Huntington Beach Navigation Center.

**Complainants.** Anthony Michael DiMarcello III and Elizabeth Tina Petruccio, both residents of the Navigation Center from 2023 to February 2024.

**The discriminatory practice.** The shelter exited any resident hospitalized longer than seven days and barred that resident from returning for thirty days. Ms. Petruccio is disabled. She was hospitalized on January 12, 2024 for right pleural empyema, sepsis, and acute respiratory failure, and was exited while in intensive care. Her hospital social worker recorded on January 19, 2024: "[s]he did not return within a week and has been exited from the Huntington Beach Navigation Center program. She must wait 30 days before she can return."

**The requested accommodation.** A modification of the seven-day exit rule so that a disability-related hospitalization is not treated as a voluntary absence, and reinstatement without the thirty-day bar.

**Why this is a fair housing matter.** The rule operates on disabled residents specifically, because only they are likely to be hospitalized for more than seven days. It is a policy, in writing, applied to a protected class, in a housing program receiving public funds.

I request an investigation and, if the facts support it, a determination and conciliation.

Respectfully,
Anthony Michael DiMarcello III
[contact]

*Note: HUD accepts complaints within one year of the discriminatory act; where the policy is continuing, the clock may run from its continued application. State the most recent application.*
