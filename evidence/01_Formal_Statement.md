## 1. Why this statement exists

I am writing this because my mother and I lived through something that is documented in pieces across hospitals, a federal courtroom, a city's files, and my mother's telephone. Nobody has put it in one place in plain language, in order, in our own voices. That is what this is.

I am not asking anyone to take my word for anything that can be checked. Where a document exists, I name it. Where the document does not exist, I say so. Where I am telling you what I believe rather than what I can prove, I say that too, in those words.

## 3. What we are asking for

I want to be precise, because a vague request gets a vague answer. We are asking for six things:

1. **A medical and environmental causation review** conducted by someone who is not employed by the City of Huntington Beach, by Mercy House, or by any entity named in the federal litigation — and that the review be given my mother's full hospital record and the site's environmental reports, not a summary of them.
2. **Written confirmation of what is under that floor and what is under that site**, including the soil gas, the groundwater, and the terms of the asphalt cap, produced by the agency that imposed the cap condition.
3. **A reasonable-accommodation determination in writing** for my mother and for me, so that the next time either of us is hospitalized we are not punished for it.
4. **Legal representation.** We have been unable to obtain counsel willing to take this on. I am asking any attorney or legal services organization reading this to evaluate it.
5. **Protection from retaliation.** I have been arrested once in my life, on a warrant mailed to an address I had not lived at since 2021, after I made disclosures to federal and state officials. I am asking that this be reviewed as what I believe it is.
6. **Housing.** My mother and I are the only two people who were at that shelter from the beginning who were never moved into supportive housing. I am asking why, in writing.

## 5. The shelter — what it was

We entered the Huntington Beach Navigation Center together. It is a congregate shelter inside a Sprung structure, built on a former industrial and oilfield parcel at 17631 Cameron Lane, adjacent to 17642 Beach Boulevard.

I want to describe it as it was, not as it was described to us.

The floor inside the sleeping area was a blue-painted asphalt surface. Along the perimeter wall, anchor bolts ran through that surface. In November 2023, my mother photographed what was happening under her own bed: the bolts were corroded, the cap was failing, the seal was taped, and raw sand and soil were coming up onto the floor where people slept. She swept it up with a broom and dustpan. I have those photographs. They are time-stamped, geotagged, and were taken on her tablet at 33.706389, -117.988611, between 12:20 and 12:36 p.m. on November 7, 2023.

The site had a two-inch asphalt cap. The City's own construction contract with TTS Engineering, Inc. — City document 20-9041/235642, Laserfiche #5406552, amended to $2,410,716.24 — warranted that grading and paving work for **one year** after acceptance, and the contractor's proposal states the crew installing it would not be 40-hour HAZWOPER trained. The County's briefing document states that the site was approved for shelter use **only as long as an asphalt pavement cover was placed over the soil**. Those two documents are the heart of this. One says the cover is the only thing protecting people. The other says the cover was warranted for a single year by an untrained crew.

The shelter rules restricted when we could leave, when we could walk, and when we could go to the pharmacy. The pharmacy was a 30-minute window at 5:00 p.m. Hospitalization of more than seven days meant automatic exit from the program and a 30-day bar on returning. My mother was exited while she was in an intensive care unit.

## 6. My mother's illness

My mother was hospitalized at Hoag Hospital Newport Beach in November 2023. She was admitted to Scripps Mercy Hospital on January 12, 2024 and remained until February 17, 2024 — thirty-six days. She was then transferred to Mission Hills Post Acute Care from February 17 to March 14, 2024 — twenty-six more days.

Her diagnoses, as recorded:

- Atypical pneumonia, J18.9, with a CT chest on January 16, 2024 showing right upper lobe ground-glass opacity with a reverse-halo sign and bilateral cavitary disease;
- Right pleural empyema — the pleural fluid showed 103,752 white blood cells, 94% neutrophils, and glucose below 20 — requiring a right chest tube;
- Sepsis, A41.9;
- Acute hypercapnic respiratory failure, J96.02, requiring intubation;
- Cervical discitis-osteomyelitis at C4–C5, M46.22, with a contrast MRI on January 26, 2024 showing prevertebral edema tracking from the anterior mediastinum up the C2–C7 prevertebral space.

She was on six antimicrobials at peak, roughly 5.25 grams per day, and then a fifty-two-day intravenous ceftriaxone and vancomycin course through a PICC line.

She weighed 160 pounds in August 2021. She weighed 116 pounds in that hospital bed.

**What her own medical record says about where she was living:** On January 13, 2024, a nurse recorded that her family reported she had been staying in a homeless shelter in Huntington Beach (page 1051). On January 15, 2024, a licensed clinical social worker wrote, in capital letters, **"SHELTER HISTORY: HB Navigation Center"** (page 1034). On January 19, 2024, a social worker recorded that she and her son had been staying together at the Navigation Center and that she had been exited from the program and must wait thirty days before returning (page 985).

**What her own medical record says about what her doctors knew:** across more than twenty-five physician progress notes, pulmonary and infectious disease consults, and the discharge summary, there is no mention of the Navigation Center at all. Instead the same sentence repeats: the patient "had been squatting with friends" and had "not had a stable home." The intake box was checked "homeless, unsheltered."

I am not a doctor and I am not accusing any physician of bad faith. I am saying that the one fact that connected her lungs to her environment was written down by social workers, three times, and never appeared in a single physician note. Whatever the reason, the connection was never evaluated.

## 7. My own injuries during the same period

- **March 24 and 28, 2023, Hoag Hospital Newport Beach.** Chest CT showed a 1.2 cm left upper lobe peribronchial density described as probably infectious or inflammatory given associated bronchiectasis, a 1.8 cm right lower lobe infiltrate, and four fractured left ribs (3rd through 6th). I returned four days later with cough, shortness of breath, a heart rate of 117, and the sensation of filling up with fluid. I was diagnosed with a pleural effusion.
- **February 11, 2024, Hoag Emergency Department.** I was brought by ambulance for a non-healing wound and staph infection of my left hip and leg. I was discharged on cephalexin 500 mg four times daily and Bactrim DS twice daily. A social work note that day records that I had been exited from the shelter and was seeking help getting back in.
- **August 29 to December 5, 2024.** A comminuted fracture of the surgical neck and head of my left humerus with posterior dislocation and hemarthrosis. Closed reduction under conscious sedation at Arrowhead Regional on September 5, 2024. Orthopedics recommended fixation. I did not receive it. My left arm was in a sling and my range of motion is permanently impaired.
- **December 14, 2025, UCI Medical Center.** Syncope and collapse, a 3 cm occipital scalp laceration repaired with six staples, and closed head injury. This occurred after I was taken into custody on the 2023 warrant described below and placed on an involuntary psychiatric hold, where I was given medication and collapsed.

The pattern I am describing is not that we each got unlucky. It is that six people who slept in the same structure in the same fourteen months were hospitalized with pneumonia, pleural disease, sepsis, or soft-tissue infection. Their names are in the record: Jesse Knabb, my mother, me, Burt Bell, Brian Bell, and Curtis Simcox.

## 9. What I believe, and what I can prove

I want this section to be exact, because it is the section that will be tested.

**What can be proven with documents:**

- The site contains hexavalent chromium at concentrations the state's own screening levels treat as requiring controls. EEC Environmental's Phase II report dated August 3, 2020 recorded up to 980 µg/kg — 49 times the DTSC residential screening level of 20 µg/kg and 3.27 times the commercial level of 300 µg/kg — alongside lead at 84.2 mg/kg, arsenic at 9.36 mg/kg, toxaphene between 500 and 1,600 µg/kg, and 4,4'-DDE at 3,100 µg/kg.
- The County conditioned shelter occupancy on an asphalt cover.
- The cover was installed under a one-year warranty.
- My mother photographed that cover failing beneath her bed on November 7, 2023, with time, date, and GPS coordinates embedded in the files.
- Six residents of that structure were hospitalized with pulmonary, pleural, septic, or soft-tissue disease in the same period.
- My mother's social workers documented the shelter; her physicians did not.
- My mother was exited from the shelter while hospitalized and barred from returning for thirty days.
- I made the disclosures listed above, in writing, on the dates listed.
- A federal court case exists: *Knabb v. City of Huntington Beach et al.*, No. 8:26-cv-00348-JWH-ADS, with an operative second amended complaint at ECF 115 and a combined opposition at ECF 130.

**What I believe but cannot yet prove to a scientific or legal standard:**

- That the specific dust and vapor from that specific floor caused my mother's specific infection and my specific lung findings. This is my honest belief. It is supported by timing, by the photographs, by the site chemistry, and by the fact that six people in one structure got the same class of illness. It has not been established by a physician or an exposure scientist. That is exactly the review I am asking for in section 3.
- That the failures I describe were the result of deliberate choices rather than negligence, indifference, or bureaucratic drift. I do not know what was in anyone's mind. I know what the documents say and what the outcomes were.
- That the warrant and the arrest were retaliatory. It is my belief. I am asking for an investigation.

**What I am not saying:**

- I am not saying any physician intended harm.
- I am not saying any named individual committed a crime.
- I am not saying this document proves any claim. It is a record of what exists and what we say happened.
