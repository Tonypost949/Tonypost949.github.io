## 3. Forensic Significance to Environmental Exposure Claims

1. **Direct Breach of the "Asphalt Cap / No Exposure Pathway" Premise:**
   - In the **July 30, 2020 EEC Environmental Limited Phase II Site Investigation** ([geotracker_17631_cameron_full_text.txt](file:///C:/OsintNeoAi/evidence/geotracker_17631_cameron_full_text.txt)), shallow soil samples (`B1`–`B12` at 0.5–5 ft bgs) confirmed detections of **Hexavalent Chromium (`200–980 µg/kg`, ~3.3× DTSC residential screening level of `300 µg/kg`)**, **Lead**, **Arsenic**, **Chlordane/Organochlorine Pesticides**, and **PCBs**.
   - EEC and OCHCA justified no further soil remediation on the premise that the **paved asphalt surface acted as an intact physical cap** preventing shelter residents from coming into direct contact with or inhaling particulate dust from shallow soil.
2. **Physical Intrusion Under Resident Beds:**
   - Photos `01`–`07` show **heavy, corroded steel anchor bolts driven directly through the blue-painted floor slab/asphalt cap** along the interior perimeter wall directly beneath resident beds (`photo_05`, `photo_07`), with makeshift duct tape peeling away (`photo_02`, `photo_07`) and **piles of raw, loose sand/soil actively spilling onto the sleeping area floor** (`photo_01`, `photo_06`).
