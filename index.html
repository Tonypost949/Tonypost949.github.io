The Trinity · three checks

Slow down. Check all three.

One dramatic claim is not the same as a verified finding. Every lead gets checked against reality, its sources, and the longer record.

GATE 01  /  PHYSICAL REALITY

Could it have happened?

Check dates, locations, distances, sequence, and original records. If the physical facts do not fit, stop and investigate why.

GATE 02  /  ODDS

What backs it up?

Record the source. Assess reliability and independent corroboration. Show uncertainty; do not dress a single unverified account up as fact.

GATE 03  /  BASELINE

Does the pattern hold?

Compare what happened with historical frequency, ordinary baselines, and changes over time. Separate a real shift from a temporary spike.
