## SECTION 2: VACATUR OF VOID ORDERS, EXTRINSIC FRAUD & SEWER SERVICE
### Controlling Statutory Authority: California Code of Civil Procedure § 473(d); Fed. R. Civ. P. 60(d)(3); 18 U.S.C. § 1708

| Precedent Case | Citation & Court | Legal Rule Established | Exact Desired Outcome Matched |
|---|---|---|---|
| ***Hazel-Atlas Glass Co. v. Hartford-Empire Co.*** | **322 U.S. 238 (1944)**<br>United States Supreme Court | Federal courts possess an inherent, permanent equitable power to vacate judgments procured through fraud upon the court. Equitable relief against fraudulent judgments is never barred by laches, statutes of limitation, or clean hands. | **Outcome 2:** Direct federal vacatur and injunction restraining enforcement of all state court summary possession writs obtained via fabricated affidavits or concealed evidence. |
| ***United States v.
